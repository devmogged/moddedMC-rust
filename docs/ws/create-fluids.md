# Create fluids

The reference is Create 6.0.10 for NeoForge 1.21.1, tag `mc1.21.1-6.0.10`.

- WS-2 registration and pressure landed in `d22614c`. The fluid-state branch includes the original fluids branch, including the regression fix for multiple faces of one fluid handler. The four `ws2a-*` scenarios cover pipe placement, orientation, pump speed and pressure after restart.
- A powered pump moves water or lava between 8,000 mB single-block tanks. In Creative, buckets add or remove one bucket and the wrench switches the tank windows. Tank contents and window state survive restart. The fluid models load from the owner's Create JAR. The bot verified the data and interactions, and a real client still needs to check the static tank models.
- To reproduce the transfer check, build and package Create, then run `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-tank.txt -RunDir run/create-fluid-tank`. Wait for the motor's block entity to register, then confirm zero RPM before filling the sending tank. The bot checks that the receiver stays empty until the motor starts, then moves 1,000 mB through two pipes, reloads the receiver and verifies that it refuses lava while it holds water. A second transfer succeeds once the water is emptied.
- In survival, apply a copper ingot to a stripped log or wood and craft the casing with iron bars to make an item drain. Right-clicking the drain with a full bucket swaps it for an empty one. The 1,500 mB buffer rejects partial exchanges, and pipes can extract through the sides or bottom. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-item-drain.txt` checks crafting, bucket exchange, transfer of 1,000 mB and persistence on both sides of the transfer.
- The spout takes fluid from pipes on its top and sides and fills containers on depots or belts. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-spout.txt` verifies that a water bottle consumes 250 mB, that a bucket waits for 1,000 mB through a restart, and that a belt carries a filled bottle to the receiving depot. The bot checks the bottle's water component. Custom filling recipes and spout animation are pending.
- The fluid valve keeps perpendicular pipe and shaft axes. Positive rotation opens it and negative rotation closes it when the pointer reaches its limit. The pointer and open state persist when stopped. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-valve.txt` verified closed flow, transfer of 1,000 mB when open, the saved pointer and a reversed closure. The static open and closed models still need a real-client check.
- Belts feed filled containers into the item drain. Dropped water bottles also enter the drain directly. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-item-drain-belt.txt` checks a dropped bottle returning 250 mB and one glass bottle, and a belt-fed bucket waiting across restart until a pump frees capacity. The receiving depot gets one empty bucket and the receiving tank holds both buckets' water.
- Tanks join into square stacks up to 3 × 3 × 32. One controller owns the fluid, and every part exposes the same inventory to pipes and Creative buckets. The wrench toggles all windows together. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-tank-multiblock.txt` moves 9,000 mB from an upper member of a two-block stack into a non-controller part of a 2 × 2 × 2 tank spanning chunk boundaries. After restart, removing the controller leaves 8,000 mB and 1,000 mB in the surviving bottom parts and a 32,000 mB empty tank on top.
- Use a tank on the top or bottom of a wide tank to fill the layer in one click. An unobstructed 2 × 2 layer consumes four tanks in Survival. Sneaking, insufficient items, or an occupied layer permits normal single-block placement. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-tank-multiplace.txt -RunDir run/create-fluid-tank-multiplace` checks all four layer positions and all eight held tanks when the player is standing in the first target. It also checks successful top and bottom placement across restart. [Issue #11](https://github.com/devmogged/moddedMC-rust/issues/11)
- Open pipes pull from fluid sources, waterlogged blocks, and full cauldrons. An outlet places a source block when its buffer reaches 1,000 mB. The buffer survives restart. Run `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-open-end.txt` to check that an isolated source dries up, 900 mB reaches a replacement tank after restart, a slab can be waterlogged and drained again, a full cauldron empties, and an infinite pool keeps its source. The same scenario verifies that lava forms stone on contact with a world water source before the pipe can discharge lava into an empty cell.
- `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-open-end-nether.txt` checks that a bucket of water leaves the tank and evaporates at a Nether outlet.
- Water and lava meeting inside a junction break the pipe and leave cobblestone. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-fluid-pipe-collision.txt` drives both pumps at 128 RPM and checks that the cobblestone remains after restart.
- Hose pulleys lower under positive rotation, stop at obstructions, and retract when rotation reverses. A pump can draw from the nozzle or send fluid back into the world. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-hose-pulley.txt -RunDir run/create-hose-pulley` checks shaft-side placement, an obstructed descent, a finite source, a 900 mB buffer and four-block offset across restart, refill, and the hose and nozzle displays appearing and retracting. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-hose-pulley-bottomless.txt -RunDir run/create-hose-pulley-bottomless` draws 8,000 mB from an 11,200-block pool with vanilla source conversion disabled, then pumps it back after restart.
- Mounted tanks and visible fluid levels remain open. The hose and nozzle use stock-client block displays. The official client still needs a visual check for the reel axis and hose alignment during motion.
