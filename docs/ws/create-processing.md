# Create basin processing

The reference is Create 6.0.10 for NeoForge 1.21.1, tag `mc1.21.1-6.0.10`.

- The basin collects item entities and saves separate nine-slot input and output inventories. Empty-hand use returns the contents. A vertical cog drives a mechanical mixer two blocks above the basin. The mixer starts at 30 RPM and keeps its recipe and stroke progress in the block entity.
- `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-mixer.txt -RunDir run/create-mixer` drops three andesite and two iron nuggets into the basin. At 16 RPM, the inputs survive a restart. At 64 RPM, the mixer produces two andesite alloy and leaves one andesite. The bot collects all three items and checks that removing the mixer removes its two displays.
- For the real-client check, place a mixer two blocks above a basin, power it with a vertical cog at 64 RPM and toss andesite and an iron nugget into the basin. Check that the whisk lowers into the basin, spins, then retracts. Retrieve one andesite alloy with an empty hand.
- The basin has two 1,000 mB input tanks and two output tanks. Pipes and containers share the same storage. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-mixer-fluid.txt -RunDir run/create-mixer-fluid` fills one input from a tank, verifies a full water input refuses another bucket, adds lava to the second input, and checks that both fluids survive restart. The mixer consumes 1,000 mB of water to make dough, leaves the lava available to pump out, and then makes mud from coarse dirt and a 250 mB water bottle. The mud recipe uses the renamed 26.3 block tag.
- A player can capture a blaze with an empty burner at its spawner. A placed burner accepts furnace fuel, returns fuel containers and persists its fuel and duration. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-blaze-burner.txt -RunDir run/create-blaze-burner` captures and places the burner in Survival, checks that coal unlocks 1,000 mB of honey and keeps its heat after restart, and checks that a lava bucket returns an empty bucket. Cobblestone stays in the basin until a blaze cake superheats it, then produces 50 mB of lava.
- The stock pack uses a vanilla cauldron for the burner cage and the Create blaze head for each heat state. The official client still needs a visual check after applying coal and a blaze cake.
- A powered press compacts basin recipes and the spout reads item-and-fluid filling recipes. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-blaze-cake.txt -RunDir run/create-blaze-cake` presses an egg, sugar and cinder flour into one blaze cake base. A spout keeps the base and its 249 mB of lava through restart. At 250 mB it consumes the lava and produces one cake on the depot.
- Zinc nuggets craft into ingots, and regular and blast furnaces cook crushed zinc. A heated mixer turns one copper and one zinc ingot into two brass ingots. Using brass on stripped logs or stripped wood makes brass casing. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-brass.txt -RunDir run/create-brass` checks the crafting table, both furnaces, the cold basin across restart, the heated result, and casing placement and loot in Survival.
- New Overworld chunks generate zinc from Y -63 to 70 using the ore placement from Create 6.0.10. An iron pickaxe yields raw zinc, and Silk Touch yields the ore block. Both ores and raw zinc can be smelted or blasted. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-zinc-ore.txt -RunDir run/create-zinc-ore` checks generated stone and deepslate ores, block identity after restart, and both mining drops with a Survival bot.
- Chutes and andesite funnels can insert into a basin and extract from either inventory. Input slots are offered first. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-basin-automation.txt -RunDir run/create-basin-automation` feeds a basin from a chest, checks partial insertion at the slot limit, drains the ingredients into a chest after restart, and collects two mixer outputs through a side funnel.
- Sequenced assemblies can drive spouts and presses. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-sturdy-sheet.txt -RunDir run/create-sturdy-sheet` spends 500 mB of lava on powdered obsidian, moves the intermediate through a player inventory, and keeps the first press stroke across restart. The second stroke finishes one sturdy sheet and clears the assembly progress.
- Shapeless and brewing recipes, automatic basin spouts and visible basin fluid levels remain open. So do live-entity blaze capture, empty cage placement and the original burner cage models.

- Sand Paper and Red Sand Paper polish one offhand item or a dropped item at the player's ray hit in 32 ticks. Releasing returns the reserved ingredient. Eight completed Survival uses break the tool. In-progress input stays on the tool across restart.
- `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-sandpaper.txt -RunDir run/create-sandpaper` checks both input paths, early release, restart and resume, Red Sand Paper breaking on the eighth use, and crafting an Electron Tube and four Train Signals. For the real-client check, hold Sand Paper with Rose Quartz in the offhand, release early once, then hold use until one Polished Rose Quartz appears. Check the animation, returned input and durability bar. Sandpaper block scraping, waxing removal and deployer automation are still pending.

- A downward Deployer runs timed recipes on depots and belts. Use the front of the Deployer to swap its held ingredient. Redstone prevents a new stroke. Cutting shaft power pauses an active stroke, and restart preserves its timer and ingredient. Breaking the Deployer drops anything it is holding.
- `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-deployer.txt -RunDir run/create-deployer` checks Survival placement, the Brass Hand, Deployer and Train Controls recipes, redstone locking, and resuming a 15-step Precision Mechanism assembly after restart. The recipe keeps the release's weighted outputs. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-deployer-belt.txt -RunDir run/create-deployer-belt` passes dirt through untouched and spends one plank to put a cogwheel on a receiving depot, with both inventories checked after restart.
- Chutes and funnels can fill and empty a Deployer's held stack. The handler merges only matching items and components up to the stack limit. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-deployer-automation.txt -RunDir run/create-deployer-automation` feeds from a chest, tops up through a funnel, rejects a different plank type, consumes one plank and drains the remainder into a chest after restart.
- Deployers also apply casing recipes to a matching block two blocks in front. `scripts/scenario.ps1 -Scenario tests/scenarios/create-deployer-casing.txt -RunDir run/create-deployer-casing` pauses midway through a stroke, changes its target to granite after restart, and checks that both sturdy sheets are preserved. Restoring the valid target twice uses one sheet per brass casing and produces two Railway Casings.
- In a real client, place a downward Deployer two blocks above a depot and power its shaft. Feed a plank into the Deployer through a chute and put a shaft on the depot. Check the body, shaft rotation and the cogwheel appearing on the depot. Hand animation, filters, sanding and general fake-player actions on blocks or entities remain open.
